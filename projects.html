Robotics · Computer Vision

Vision-Guided Cartesian Pick-and-Place Robot

Raspberry Pi · YOLOv8 · OpenCV · Arduino · A4988 · React

A 3-axis Cartesian gantry robot that detects, classifies and localises coloured shapes using a custom-trained YOLOv8 model, converts their pixel coordinates into workspace millimetres via a homography calibration, and autonomously picks and places them. NEMA 17 steppers are driven through A4988 drivers from an Arduino, with a Python control loop running on the Raspberry Pi. A web control interface was built using React, JavaScript, HTML and CSS. Critical mechanical parts — stepper motor holders, linear actuator holder — were designed in AutoCAD.

Embedded · IoT · Signal Processing

Smart Bicycle Instrumentation System

Dual ESP32 · MAX30102 · MPU6050 · NEO-6M GPS · TCRT5000 · Firebase

A sensor-based rider-monitoring and IoT system that captures heart rate (MAX30102), motion and orientation (MPU6050), GPS position (NEO-6M) and a custom-built reflective IR wheel-speed sensor, all streamed to Firebase from two ESP32 boards communicating over ESP-NOW. Sensor calibration and signal filtering — moving average, 1D Kalman, complementary filter and threshold peak detection — were implemented in C++ for reliable real-time readings. A 4" LVGL touchscreen on the bike shows live data; four web dashboards display history for health, motion, GPS and velocity.

SAUVC 2026 — Autonomous Underwater Vehicle

MPU9250 · QMC5883L · Pressure transducer · Madgwick · Team Diyakawa

An untethered autonomous underwater vehicle built by Team Diyakawa for the Singapore AUV Challenge 2026. The qualifying video was accepted and the team was shortlisted for the competition. My contribution was sensor integration — the MPU9250 IMU, QMC5883L magnetometer and a pressure transducer for depth — including calibration routines for gyro bias, accelerometer offsets and magnetometer hard/soft-iron distortion, plus a full Madgwick AHRS implementation for roll, pitch and yaw estimation. Mechanical CAD is maintained in the team's shared repository.

Manufacturing Technology

Multi-Purpose Device Holder

Woodworking · Sheet metal · Welding · Metalworking

A compact multi-purpose holder for laptop, tablet, phone and headset, fabricated from a combination of wood and metal components. Woodworking included cutting, planing, drilling, chiselling, sanding and mortise-and-tenon joint fabrication for the main frame. Metal components were produced using rod bending, sheet-metal bending, welding and spot welding to build adjustable holders. Hinges, U-shaped support rods, an aluminium L-bar and a wing-nut adjustment mechanism give the holder its adjustability. Final finishing included grinding, sanding, sealing and painting. Gained practical experience in process selection, workshop tools, material joining and fabrication methods.

Materials · Reverse Engineering

Reverse Engineering of an Infrared Cooker

Material characterisation · Sheet-metal forming · SolidWorks

Comprehensive reverse engineering analysis of the metallic cover assembly of an infrared cooker, focusing on material identification, manufacturing process evaluation and design improvement. Material characterisation combined visual inspection, spark testing, density measurement, magnetic testing and Optical Emission Spectrometry (OES), identifying the material as low carbon steel (AISI 1006–1010). The deep-drawing process was analysed with respect to clearance, radii and structural integrity. Detailed 3D models and production drawings of the cover, punch and die assembly were produced in SolidWorks. Proposed improvements include reinforcement ribs for stiffness and thermal stability, and hemmed/rolled edges for safety and finish quality.

Biped Wall-Climbing Robot

Mechatronic System Design · 5-DoF · Suction adhesion · DH kinematics

Design of a 5-DoF biped wall-climbing robot with suction-based adhesion for vertical wall inspection. The design uses a negative-pressure adhesion system and a two-legged inchworm gait for controlled vertical locomotion. A Denavit-Hartenberg kinematic model and inverse kinematics are being developed for 5-DoF joint control and suction-unit positioning. Planned sensor integration includes cameras, ToF sensors, encoders, IMU and pressure sensors for perception, feedback, orientation and adhesion monitoring.

Status: CAD design complete · Prototype build and control integration pending

Ongoing · Simulation

6-DoF Robot Arm — Pick and Place Simulation

EN3563 Robotics · Gazebo · ROS2 · DH kinematics · Trajectory planning

A pick-and-place task for a 6-DoF industrial robot arm in the Gazebo simulation environment. The work covers coordinate frame assignment, Denavit-Hartenberg parameter derivation, forward and inverse kinematics, and trajectory planning that maintains the object's upright orientation throughout the motion. A smooth trapezoidal velocity profile is used for controlled joint movement. I designed my own 6-DoF arm as a learning platform, then applied that experience to the YASKAWA GP7 model used for the actual simulation.

Status: Simulation in progress · Kinematics and trajectory planning underway
