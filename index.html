Vision-Guided Cartesian Robot

Raspberry Pi · YOLOv8 · Arduino · A4988

A 3-axis Cartesian pick-and-place robot that detects and classifies coloured shapes with a custom YOLOv8 model, computes their workspace coordinates, and autonomously picks and places them. Includes a web-based control interface.

GitHub →

Smart Bicycle Instrumentation

Dual ESP32 · MAX30102 · MPU6050 · NEO-6M

An ESP32-based instrumentation system for a bicycle — heart rate, IMU, GPS and custom wheel-speed sensing streamed to Firebase, with a live LVGL touchscreen on the bike and four web dashboards for analysis.

GitHub →

SAUVC 2026 — AUV

MPU9250 · QMC5883L · Pressure sensor · Madgwick

An untethered autonomous underwater vehicle for the Singapore AUV Challenge 2026. Video submission accepted and team shortlisted. My contribution: IMU, magnetometer and pressure sensor integration, plus a Madgwick-based sensor fusion pipeline.
